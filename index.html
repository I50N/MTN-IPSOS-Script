Important notice
Introduction — Siswati
Read the following introduction in Siswati to the respondent:
Sawubona, ligama lami ngingu .... [Ligama Lakho], futsi ngishayela lucingo ngivela e-Ipsos, inkampani letimele yeLucwaningo lweMakethe.
Senta luhlolo egameni lenkhampani yetekuchumana ngekushaya lucingo mayelana nekwenetiseka kwakho ngemasevisi akho anyalo e voice, data ne masevisi etetimali.
Tonkhe timphendvulo titawusetjentiselwa lubalo kuphela, futsi asizami kukutsengisela lutfo.
Tonkhe tinkhulumomphendvulwano titawu recordwa ngenhloso yekucecesha kanye nekulawula lizinga leliphakeme, futsi ngeke titsatse sikhatsi sakho lesingetulu kwemaminutes lelishumi nesihlanu (15).
Ngingachubeka yini?
Response Handling

If YES / Uma atsi Yebo:

Proceed to the survey on the client CRM system.

If NO / Uma atsi Cha:

Thank the respondent politely and close the call. Log the outcome in the CRM as "Declined".

If respondent asks who gave their number:

Linombolo lakho latsatfwa kusuka kurekodi yabakhathaleti be-MTN Eswatini njengenkhomba yekutsi usebentisa emasevisi abo.
Translation: Your number was drawn from MTN Eswatini's customer records as an indication that you use their services.
English Translation (Reference)
Hello, my name is [Your Name], and I am calling from Ipsos, an independent Market Research company.
We are conducting a survey on behalf of a telecommunications company regarding your satisfaction with your current voice, data and financial services.
All responses will be used for statistical purposes only, and we are not trying to sell you anything.
All conversations will be recorded for training and quality control purposes, and it will not take more than fifteen (15) minutes of your time.
May I proceed?
